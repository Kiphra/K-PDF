K-PDF: PDF Editor & Tools is a comprehensive, offline-first PDF and image manipulation powerhouse for Android, developed and published by Kiphra.

Features include:
* Merge multiple PDFs into one seamless document
* Split PDF into separate individual documents
* Compress PDF with granular quality presets to reduce file size
* Scan documents to PDF using camera with edge perspective crop
* Convert PDF pages to high-resolution images & Images to PDF
* Reorder, rotate, delete, and extract specific pages
* Digital signatures & watermarking (text or logo)
* Password protect PDFs (encrypt with 128/256-bit security & unlock)
* On-device OCR text recognition & extraction
* View and edit PDF metadata (title, author, subject, keywords)
* Advanced Print Imposition (booklet formatting, N-Up layout)
* Image editing tools (compress, crop, resize, convert formats)
* Privacy-first offline architecture: documents never leave your device
