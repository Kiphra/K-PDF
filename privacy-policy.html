01 Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the K-PDF: PDF Editor & Tools mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "K-PDF", "the App", or "we"), developed and published by Kiphra.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your sensitive data. K-PDF is built from the ground up on an offline-first, on-device architecture. This policy details how our application interacts with your device and handles data.

02 Document Processing & Storage

ZERO CLOUD STORAGE   LOCAL EXECUTION

Your documents and PDFs are among the most sensitive files you own. K-PDF operates under the following strict guarantees:

  • Local Document Engine: All PDF manipulations (merging, splitting, compressing, watermarking, signing, form filling, OCR text extraction, and image conversions) execute entirely within the local sandbox of your Android device.
  • No Remote Uploads: The App never uploads, transmits, or mirrors your PDF files or images to external servers or third-party cloud storage.
  • Temporary Cache Cleanup: Any temporary cache files created while rendering previews or compressing documents are stored strictly within the app's local private cache directory and can be purged at any time from the app settings.
  • Local History: Recent file references displayed in the Files tab are stored locally on your device's private SQLite database. You can clear this history anytime.

03 Device Permissions

K-PDF requests only the minimal permissions necessary to provide its core PDF manipulation capabilities:

  • Storage / Media Files: Required to allow you to select documents from your device and to save newly generated PDFs to your local storage or Downloads folder. Access is restricted strictly to files you choose.
  • Camera (Optional): Required solely if you utilize the "Scan to PDF" tool. Captured images are processed entirely on-device and are never uploaded or transmitted.
  • Internet Access: Used for optional network features such as checking for updates, rendering third-party advertisements, or viewing legal web pages. Document files never utilize this connection.

04 Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

To support ongoing development and offer core PDF utilities for free, K-PDF may display non-intrusive advertisements served by trusted third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob):

  • Advertising Identifiers: Third-party ad networks may process standard device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID), coarse location, or general device metrics solely to serve and measure ads in compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies.
  • Document Data Isolation: Under no circumstance do advertising SDKs have access to your PDF documents, file names, file contents, camera feed, or extracted OCR text.
  • Ad Personalization Controls: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time directly through your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID).

05 Third-Party Open Source Software

K-PDF utilizes trusted third-party software components and libraries under their respective open-source licenses (such as Apache 2.0, MIT, and BSD), including PdfBox-Android, Google ML Kit, Glide, uCrop, and Jetpack Compose.

These components run locally on your device and are used solely for UI rendering, image cropping, and PDF manipulation. Full legal attributions and license texts can be inspected inside the App under Settings → Third-Party Notices or on our Third-Party Notices page.

06 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

K-PDF is designed as a general-purpose productivity utility and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe any data has inadvertently been collected, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

07 User Rights & Data Deletion

In accordance with GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation), CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act), and global privacy frameworks:

  • Because your files reside strictly on your device, you have immediate, unilateral control over all your data.
  • You can delete all local data, cached PDFs, and recent history at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or using the in-app "Clear Cache" button.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all application-private caches and settings.
